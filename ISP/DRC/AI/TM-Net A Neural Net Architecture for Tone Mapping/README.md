# TM-Net: A Neural Net Architecture for Tone Mapping

## Abstract
> This paper makes three contributions.
> - First, we show thatCLHE can be exactly formulated as a deep tone mapping neural network (which we call the TM-Net).
> - Second, we show that we can train a fixed 2-layer TM-Net to compute CLHE, thereby making CLHE up to 30× faster to compute.
> - Thirdly, we take a more complex tone-mapper (that uses quadratic programming) and show that it too can also be implemented — without loss of visual accuracy—using a bespoke trained 2-layer TM-Net.
